# Copy

Words are the first thing that gives a generated page away, because a model's first draft is always the same draft.

## Rules

- **Say what it does, for whom, in nouns and verbs.** "Turns a one-hour meeting into five decisions you can search" beats "Reimagine how your team collaborates".
- **Use the user's own details.** Their product names, town, prices, process. One specific fact does more than three adjectives.
- **Cut every word that any company could use.** Seamless, effortless, elevate, unlock, supercharge, powerful, next-generation, game-changing, all-in-one, built for the modern team. Also: "It's not just X, it's Y" and three-part lists that exist for rhythm.
- **Do not repeat the heading in the paragraph below it.**
- **Headlines are not slogans by default.** A plain, surprising statement is often stronger than a clever one.
- **Buttons name what happens next:** "Start a two-week trial", not "Get started", when you know which it is.

## Invented content

Do not make up customers, quotes, numbers, awards, team members, prices or contact details for a real business. Mark the slot, such as `[add a real quote]`, and list these in your reply. Vague false precision (50+ countries, 99.9% uptime, 10,000 teams) is worse than saying nothing, because in many places it is also an unlawful advertising claim.
